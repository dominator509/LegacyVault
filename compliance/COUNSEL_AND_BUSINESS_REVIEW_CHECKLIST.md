# Counsel and Business Review Checklist

No item below is approved merely because this checklist exists.

- [ ] Legal entity, business registration, address, privacy contact, and support contact verified
- [ ] Privacy Policy reconciled line by line with deployed collection, purposes, locations, retention, rights, and vendors
- [ ] Terms reconciled with pricing, refunds, disclaimers, liability, indemnity, governing law, dispute process, and insurance
- [ ] AI Processing Notice reconciled with current DeepSeek entity, terms, privacy policy, retention, training/secondary use, deletion, security, subprocessors, and transfers
- [ ] Data Processing Addendum approved and signed where required
- [ ] Data Protection Impact Assessment reviewed and residual risks accepted by named owners
- [ ] Subprocessor contracts, DPAs, legal entities, regions, and change-notice process verified
- [ ] Retention periods and legal-hold rules approved and mapped to executable controls
- [ ] Cyber and technology errors-and-omissions insurance evidence approved
- [ ] Written data-broker determination approved
- [ ] US-only launch boundary and jurisdiction-specific rights/appeals confirmed
- [ ] Emergency-access language, authority limits, delay, fraud handling, and pilot approved
- [ ] Copyright, upload authority, voice/likeness, minors, medical-summary, financial-category, and end-of-life content treatment approved
- [ ] Production policy versions, hashes, publication URLs, effective dates, prior-version archive, and acceptance evidence verified
- [ ] Incident contacts, regulatory-notice decision process, and completed exercise approved

Store approved, non-secret evidence under `compliance/evidence/` and record its
artifact hash in the release record. Do not commit restricted contracts or
personal information unless the repository's approved evidence-handling policy
explicitly permits it.
