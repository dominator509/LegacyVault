# Data Processing Addendum Template

Status: non-executable counsel-review template. It must not be signed or
published until the controller, processor, jurisdictions, vendors, locations,
and security schedule match the deployed service.

## Parties and roles

- Controller/customer legal name and address: `[REQUIRED]`
- Processor/service legal name and address: `[REQUIRED]`
- Effective date and governing agreement: `[REQUIRED]`
- Role allocation for partner organizations: `[REQUIRED]`

## Processing instructions

The processor may process personal data only to operate, secure, support, and
provide the customer-requested Legacy Vault service, on documented instructions,
and for no unrelated advertising, sale, model training, or profiling purpose.
The final agreement must define duration, data-subject categories, data types,
sensitive-data treatment, and prohibited-secret handling.

## Required processor obligations

The final agreement must address confidentiality, least privilege, encryption,
tenant isolation, incident notification, assistance with data-subject requests,
deletion and return, audit cooperation, business continuity, government
requests, subprocessor authorization and notice, and demonstrable staff-access
controls.

## International transfers

List every actual processing country and lawful transfer mechanism. Do not
assert a region, localization guarantee, or supplementary measure until it has
been verified and archived under `compliance/evidence/`.

## Subprocessors

Attach the approved version of `SUBPROCESSOR_REGISTER.md`. The production
change-notice period, objection mechanism, and replacement/termination remedy
remain `[REQUIRED COUNSEL DECISION]`.

## Security schedule

Reconcile the final schedule with `SECURITY.md`,
`compliance/CONTROL_TRACEABILITY.md`, production architecture, recovery tests,
and the vendor contracts. Marketing language is not evidence.

## Deletion and return

Use the approved `DATA_RETENTION_SCHEDULE.md`. State active-system deletion,
processor propagation, legal-hold boundaries, backup expiry, export timing, and
deletion evidence separately. Selective destructive backup editing is not
promised.

## Execution gate

Counsel approval, authorized signatures, and the final executed copy are
external evidence. Until present, this template does not satisfy the production
DPA gate.
